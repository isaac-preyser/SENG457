- How do we generate a phase oracle $O_f$? We always "recieve" an oracle (that is effectively a black box), but in real life we will need to generate these oracles 
  to encode (or mark) the solution we desire (i.e., our search term). Is there some "quantum compiler", or other algortithm to assist with this generation, or do we need
  to create bespoke oracles for every search operation we wish to perform? 

  Especially as our search space N increases, we need more and more qubits (n = log_2[N]) to facilitate a larger and larger superpostion. This means our oracle will consist of more gates put together by the
  Kronecker Product.
